ITALY / MOUNTAINS

Somewhere between the mountains & the sky.

Some trips are measured in miles. This one was measured in mornings.

We left the map folded in a pocket and followed the path until the village disappeared behind us. There was no grand destination, only the promise of a warm meal and a new view.

Room for the unexpected

Travel slowly enough and the smallest things become the story: an open window, a conversation at a crossroads, the color of the light just before evening.

Our advice is simple. Pack less. Leave space. Take the long way home.
